# Customer_churn_analysis_using_powerbi
The company is experiencing a high rate of customer churn, leading to revenue loss and reduced customer lifetime value. However, it lacks clear insights into the factors driving churn and is unable to effectively identify and retain at-risk customers.
Customer churn is a critical issue that directly impacts business revenue and growth.
The analysis identified key factors influencing churn, such as contract type, tenure, and monthly charges.
Customers with short-term contracts and higher charges are more likely to churn.
New customers show a higher tendency to leave compared to long-term customers.
Using Power BI, meaningful insights were generated through interactive visualizations.
These insights can help businesses take data-driven actions to improve customer retention.
Improve onboarding experience for new customers to reduce early churn.
Improve fiber optic service quality and customer support.
Encourage customers to move from month-to-month plans to long-term contracts through discounts and offers.
Identify high-risk customers early and provide personalized retention offers.
